## Actions

1. **Point “donne moi une couleur au hasard” at the named-colour page.** Query: donne moi une couleur au hasard. Since 7 Sep the homepage has 501 impressions, 27 clicks, 5.4% click-through, position 3.9, while `/outils/choisir-une-couleur-au-hasard` has 18 impressions and 0 clicks at position 9.6. The same window puts “choisis une couleur au hasard” on the homepage at 126 impressions, 3 clicks, 2.4%, position 4.8. The page type is already a generator: the France mobile results that repeated were other tools, not articles. The named-colour page’s first screen has the matching heading, the button, and the limit (rouge, bleu, vert, and the other named colours, no HEX). The homepage’s first screen is a generated colour and its codes, and its title still ends in “Couleur au hasard”. Remove that ending from the homepage title. Leave the named-colour title as it is. Do not move the head query “couleur au hasard” with it: that one still earns its clicks on the homepage. This splits the earlier roadmap note, which kept the whole “couleur au hasard” cluster on the homepage.

2. **Take the generator queries off the draw page.** URL: `/outils/tirage-au-sort-couleur`. Since 7 Sep, “couleur aléatoire” on that URL is 572 impressions, 15 clicks, position 5.9, against 980 impressions and 59 clicks at position 4.8 on the homepage. “couleur au hasard” on the draw URL is 208 impressions, 4 clicks, position 7.6. The draw page’s own query is healthy and should be left alone: “tirage au sort couleur” is 203 clicks from 468 impressions at position 2.3, and the homepage is no longer the page Google shows for it. The first screen is already a list and a draw, which is why those other snippets do not get clicked. The title and H1 already say “tirage au sort”. The change is to stop this URL answering the generator query in the body, so Google has one URL for “couleur aléatoire”. Do not retitle the draw page.
